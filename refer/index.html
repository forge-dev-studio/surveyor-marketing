How it works

  1. Tell us who they are below. It has to be someone you know personally: a firm you have worked with or know well, not a name off a list. We check their territory first: if they are within 65 miles of a firm we already work with, that territory is taken, and we tell you so.
  2. Eric emails them once, says you suggested it, and then calls. We never text them, and we never hand you a script to send.
  3. If they sign and pay their advance, we thank you, and we tell you it happened.

How we thank you

  • If you are a client: your own per-lead rate drops to the referral rate, starting the month their advance clears, for as long as you stay a client. Already on that rate? You get the cash thank-you instead.
  • Everyone else: a cash thank-you once their advance clears. We need a W-9 from you before we can send it.
  • The firm you send: their advance buys 100 leads instead of 50, and they keep the lower rate for as long as they stay.

The 100-lead start is for personal introductions. If a firm comes to us through your link instead of this form, we check with you that you know them before they sign. Each firm keeps its own rate whether or not the other stays. If you recommend us anywhere public, say that you get a thank-you for it.
