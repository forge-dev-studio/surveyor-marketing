How it works

  1. Call a surveying firm you know and tell them about us. It has to be a firm you know: one you have worked with or know well, not a name off a list.
  2. Bring Eric onto the call. Call (762) 999-PLAT and add them, or send the form below and Eric will call you at a good time so you can add them. Our line records calls, so once everyone is on, Eric will say so.
  3. Rather have them call us? They can call (762) 999-PLAT and tell us your firm sent them. That counts too.
  4. We check their territory: if they are within 65 miles of a firm we already work with, that territory is taken, and we tell you both.
  5. If they sign and pay their advance, your thank-you starts that month.

We never email or text a firm out of the blue. The first conversation is the one you start.

How we thank you

This is company to company: thank-yous go on your firm's account as a lower price, never as cash.

The firm you introduce

  • Their $3,000 advance covers their first 100 leads, $30 each.
  • After that, $50 a lead for as long as they stay. Their bill never goes over $5,000 a month.
  • The same guarantee as everyone: 50 leads in the 90 days after they go live, or the full $3,000 back, and every lead we sent is theirs.

Your firm

  • Still getting set up when you make the introduction (your website isn't live yet): the same start. Your $3,000 covers your first 100 leads at $30 each, and your rate is $50 a lead after that.
  • Website already live: your rate drops from $60 to $50 a lead, for as long as you stay a client.
  • Already at $50 a lead: each introduction lowers your monthly bill ceiling by $500, from $5,000 down to as low as $4,000.

Your thank-you starts the month the firm you introduce pays its advance. Each firm keeps its own price whether or not the other stays.

Not a client? Point a firm our way anyway: they still get the same start. We just have no account to thank you on. If you recommend us anywhere public, say that you get a thank-you for it.
