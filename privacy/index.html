What You Tell Us

The form on this site asks for your first and last name, your phone number, your email address, and the best time to call. The name, phone and email are required, because the whole point is to call you back. The best time to call is optional.

If you call or email instead, what you tell us there is kept and used the same way.

What Gets Collected Automatically

This site runs no analytics and sets no cookies. Two things still get recorded:

  • How you got here. When you arrive, your browser's session storage saves the campaign tags on the link you followed, the ad click IDs some platforms add to it (gclid, fbclid, msclkid), the site that referred you, and the page you landed on. If you send the form later in the same visit, your request carries them, which is how we know which of our own marketing is working. Session storage clears when you close the tab.
  • Web server records. Like any web host, ours receives the IP address of each request it serves.

How We Use It

  • To call or email you back about your request.
  • To talk through territory, pricing and what the program would do for your firm.
  • To deliver the work if you hire us.
  • To measure which of our marketing brings in firms like yours.

Who Handles It for Us

These companies handle parts of it on our behalf, each under its own terms and privacy policy:

  • Cloudflare runs the service that receives the form and passes it along.
  • Resend delivers your request to our inbox by email.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this site's files and receives the IP address of each request.

Requests from this site go to our inbox and are not loaded into a customer database. If that changes, this page changes with it.

Text Messages

Nothing on this site texts you automatically. If we reply by text to the number you gave, it is about your request, and how often depends on the conversation. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or HELP for help. You can also ask us to stop by phone or email.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Selling, Keeping and Protecting It

We don't sell your personal information.

We keep a request for as long as we need it to answer you, do the work, and keep ordinary business records, and then delete what we no longer need. We protect it with reasonable safeguards, such as password-protected accounts and encrypted connections, though no system connected to the internet is perfectly secure.

Children

This site is for surveying firms. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us something, tell us and we will delete it.

Seeing or Deleting Your Information

To see what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, email eric@georgiaseocompany.com or call (762) 999-PLAT. We may ask a question or two to confirm it's you before acting on the request.

Changes to This Policy

When this policy changes, the new version replaces this one here with its own effective date. This version took effect on September 22, 2026.
